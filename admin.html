      Add a position

      Spot is the portfolio: each position takes its weight of the $1,000,000 book. Leverage is a trade: long or short at a leverage, ROI only, no size. An order is a resting level. Everything you save is visible to members at once — whether members see The Desk at all is decided in Settings → What goes live. The price feed follows the symbol by itself.
