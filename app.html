    Good to know

    • Your seat is tied to your membership.

      While it is active, you are in every channel: every trade, every investment, every market call, in real time.

    • If a payment fails or the plan ends, the channels close for you.

      Automatically, the same day. You stop seeing the calls while the rest of the group keeps getting them. Keep the card valid and nothing changes.

    • Back on time.

      The moment a payment clears again, your links come back and you are approved in a second. Nothing to request, nobody to ask.

    • New phone or new Telegram account?

      Disconnect below and join again with fresh links. Links are personal and work once.
