Your membership.

Plan & billing

What is included

    The Desk (portfolio, economic data, earnings) opens for all members at no extra cost.

    Billing questions

    Payments are processed by Whop. Receipts, card changes and cancellations live under Manage subscription. Cancelling keeps your access until the end of the period you paid for.

    /03Telegram

    Your channels.

    Private rooms, personal links. Approved automatically, for your account only.

    Channels

    —

    How it works

    01Tap Join

    The link opens the channel in Telegram.

    02Press Join in Telegram

    It may read Request to join. Telegram sends us the request for your account.

    03Approved in a second

    The request is matched to this membership and approved. Nobody else can use your link.

    Good to know

    Your Telegram account is tied to this membership the first time a link is approved. If a membership ends, that account is removed from the channels automatically and comes back when it is active again. Changing phone? Use Disconnect Telegram and join with new links.

    Quick answers

    • My payment failed.

      Go to Membership → Update your card. Access comes back the moment the charge goes through.

    • I left a channel by mistake.

      Go to Telegram and get a new link. You are approved again in a second.

    • I changed my Telegram account.

      Telegram → Disconnect, then join with the new account.

    • I paid with a different email.

      Membership is matched by email. Write to us with your receipt and we link it to this account.

    • How do I cancel?

      Membership → Manage subscription. Access runs to the end of the paid period.
