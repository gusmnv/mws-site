Privacy policy.

What we keep about you, why, and where it lives. Short, because we keep very little.

1. What we collect

  • Account: your email address, an optional name and your password (stored hashed, never readable by us).
  • Membership: your plan, its status and renewal dates, as reported by Whop, and each payment Whop reports (amount, date, receipt, card brand and last four digits), kept to show your billing history. We keep Whop's notices about your membership and payments as Whop sends them; besides the above they can include your name and username on Whop and, when Whop has them, the billing address and phone number given at checkout, the IP address Whop saw at checkout and the card's expiry date and first digits. We never see or store full card numbers; payments are handled entirely by Whop.
  • Telegram: your Telegram user id, name and username once one of your invite links is approved, and which channels you were invited to, joined or removed from.
  • Messages: what you write to us in Feedback and our replies, and a copy of each email we send you.
  • Technical: standard server logs and an audit trail of access changes, kept for security and support.

2. Why

To deliver what you paid for: recognise your membership, give you access to the channels, remove access when a membership ends, send you the emails needed to run your account (codes, receipts, access changes) and answer support requests. We do not send marketing emails; the only other emails are occasional announcements about the service itself, sent to the email on your account.

3. Where it lives

We use a small number of providers to run the service, each only for its job: Supabase (database and login), Whop (payments and subscriptions), Resend (sending emails), Telegram (delivering channel access) and GitHub Pages (hosting the website). Pages also load fonts from Google Fonts and code from jsDelivr; inside the member area, charts load from TradingView, company pages read each company's filings directly from the SEC (EDGAR), live prices stream from our own server (hosted on Railway) and, for crypto positions, directly from Binance's public market feed, and the crypto terminal connects your browser directly to the public market feeds of the exchanges it shows (Binance, OKX, Bybit, Coinbase, Kraken, Deribit, Gate, Hyperliquid); these services see your IP address. We do not sell or rent your data and we do not run advertising trackers.

4. Cookies and storage

The website uses your browser's local storage to keep you logged in, to remember your checkout while you create your account and, in the member area, to remember your display settings and keep a cache of the data it shows on your device. No third-party advertising or analytics cookies are set by us.

5. Retention

Account and membership data is kept while you have an account and for as long as needed afterwards to handle billing disputes and legal obligations. Audit logs are kept for as long as the service runs, for security and support.

6. Your rights

You can see and change your name and password in your member area (Settings, then Account). To access, correct or delete the data we hold about you, or to close your account, write to contact@mrofwallstreet.com from the email address on the account. Deleting your account ends your membership access; it does not refund payments (see the terms).

7. Contact

Privacy questions: contact@mrofwallstreet.com.
